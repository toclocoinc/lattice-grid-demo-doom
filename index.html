WebAssembly Doom on a Lattice grid

Doom, compiled to WebAssembly, rendering into a Lattice grid instead of a canvas. Each frame row is one grid row, and its line of pixels is written as a single gradient on that row's cell through the grid's formatting rules. Every frame is diffed against the previous one, and only the rows that changed are pushed into the grid.
