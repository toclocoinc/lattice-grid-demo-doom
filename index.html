WebAssembly Doom on a Lattice grid

Doom, compiled to WebAssembly, rendering into a 160×100 grid of coloured cells instead of a canvas. Each frame is box-filtered down to the grid's resolution, diffed against the previous frame, and only the cells that changed are pushed into the grid.
